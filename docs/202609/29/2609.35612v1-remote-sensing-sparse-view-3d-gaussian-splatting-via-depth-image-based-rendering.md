## 摘要
在稀疏观测下，遥感新视角合成仍然具有挑战性，原因在于几何约束不足以及跨视角监督有限。现有的神经辐射场（NeRF）和3D高斯泼溅（3DGS）方法容易过拟合，并面临深度歧义、跨视角信息缺失以及观测不足区域约束不足等挑战。为解决这些问题，我们提出DIBR-GS，一种神经高斯泼溅框架，利用基于深度图像的渲染（DIBR）生成伪视角，以进行跨视角一致性监督。具体而言，通过将单目深度先验与稀疏SfM重建对齐来构建可靠的几何初始化，并将跨视角外观先验融入神经高斯表示，以增强稀疏观测下的外观建模。此外，我们引入一种基于DIBR的渐进式伪视角监督策略，以提供额外的几何和外观约束，从而更完整地重建弱观测区域。同时，设计了一种高度约束的锚点生长策略，以抑制不合理的高斯扩展。实验表明，在仅使用3个输入视角进行训练时，所提方法取得了优于现有方法的性能。与之前表现最佳的方法相比，PSNR提高了6.83 dB，SSIM和LPIPS的相对增益分别为14%和60%，同时保持了具有竞争力的计算效率。我们的代码可在 https://github.com/kanehub/DIBR-GS 获取

## Abstract
Remote sensing novel view synthesis under sparse observations remains challenging due to insufficient geometric constraints and limited cross-view supervision. Existing Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) and 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) methods are prone to overfitting and face challenges of depth ambiguities, missing cross-view information, and insufficient constraints in under-observed regions. To address these challenges, we propose DIBR-GS, a neural Gaussian Splatting framework that exploits Depth Image-Based Rendering (DIBR) to generate pseudo views for cross-view consistency supervision. Specifically, reliable geometric initialization is constructed by aligning monocular depth priors with sparse SfM reconstruction, and cross-view appearance priors are incorporated into neural Gaussian representations to enhance appearance modeling under sparse observations. Furthermore, we introduce a progressive DIBR-based pseudo-view supervision strategy to provide additional geometric and appearance constraints, enabling more complete reconstruction of weakly observed regions. In addition, a height-constrained anchor growth strategy is designed to suppress unreasonable Gaussian expansion. Experiments demonstrate that the proposed method achieves superior performance over existing approaches when training with only 3 input views. Compared with the previous best-performing method, it improves PSNR by 6.83 dB, with relative gains of 14\% in SSIM and 60\% in LPIPS, while maintaining competitive computational efficiency. Our code is available at https://github.com/kanehub/DIBR-GS
