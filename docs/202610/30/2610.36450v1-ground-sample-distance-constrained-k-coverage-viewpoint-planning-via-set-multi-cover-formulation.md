## 摘要
基于无人机的三维重建与巡检要求视点既满足图像质量约束，又能对每个表面区域进行多次观测，而基于重叠的规划启发式方法无法保证这一点。本文研究以表面体素表示的结构的地面采样距离（GSD）约束 k 覆盖视点选择问题。候选视点沿表面向外方向生成，经距离、视角、视场和遮挡测试筛选，并对可见性不足的体素进行增补。随后，将视点选择建模为带逐体素覆盖需求的集合多重覆盖问题。我们提出一种动态加权贪心启发式方法，优先考虑剩余覆盖缺额和稀缺的观测备选方案，并在相同候选池上将其与顺序重叠剪枝方法以及整数线性规划（ILP）模型进行比较。在两种结构、四种体素分辨率和三种覆盖要求下，所提方法以紧凑的视点集合实现完全或接近完全的 k 覆盖，仅比 ILP 多使用 3.00--16.39% 的视点，同时提供基于覆盖缺额和视点可用性的可解释选择过程。

## Abstract
Drone-based 3D reconstruction and inspection require viewpoints that satisfy image-quality constraints while observing each surface region multiple times, which overlap-based planning heuristics do not guarantee. We study ground sample distance (GSD)-constrained $k$-coverage viewpoint selection for structures represented by surface voxels. Candidate viewpoints are generated along outward surface directions, screened with distance, viewing-angle, field-of-view, and occlusion tests, and augmented for voxels with insufficient visibility. Selection is then posed as set multi-cover with per-voxel coverage demands. We propose a dynamically weighted greedy heuristic that prioritizes remaining coverage deficits and scarce viewing alternatives, and compare it with sequential overlap-based pruning and an integer linear programming (ILP) formulation on the same candidate pools. Across two structures, four voxel resolutions, and three coverage requirements, the proposed approach achieves full or near-full $k$-coverage with compact viewpoint sets, using only 3.00--16.39\% more viewpoints than ILP while offering an interpretable selection process based on coverage deficits and viewpoint availability.
